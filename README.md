# BVB Basel – Legacy Ticket Machine 3D Asset

Reference-led reconstruction based on three user-supplied photographs of a green Basel BVB street ticket machine.

**Model:** approximately 0.79 m wide, 0.49 m deep and 1.53 m high including a 0.453 m pedestal. +Z up; -Y front; -X map side. Exact manufacturer measurements were not available.

Includes the curved aluminium front face, recessed LCD, ticket slot, coin hardware, side map panel and aging/wear materials. The generated map is illustrative (not an official timetable). Noninteractive environmental prop, optimized for a game.

**Work product and validation:** 76 components, 3,868 triangles; visual inspection identified and repaired obstructed screen/slot, mirrored map, inverted text and top-face z-fighting. No certified photogrammetry/scan accuracy is claimed.

The complete textured GLB, OBJ/MTL, PBR maps, scene generator and review images were produced in the user's ChatGPT conversation. This repository will track regeneration sources and game asset exports.

Import GLB into Blender/Unreal Engine, keep -Y as the front and verify scale (metres).
